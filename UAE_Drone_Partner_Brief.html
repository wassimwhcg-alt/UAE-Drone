01 — The Opportunity

Why the UAE. Why Now.

The UAE has committed AED 1 billion ($272M) to drone technology development through the UAE Drone Strategy. Commercial drone registrations grew 53% in Dubai alone last year. This is an infrastructure build — and every infrastructure build needs operators on the ground first.

The market isn't theoretical. There are 900,000+ solar panels at the Mohammed bin Rashid Solar Park alone, all requiring regular inspection. Real estate developers in Dubai spend tens of thousands on aerial surveys every month. Construction firms need weekly progress documentation.

As a certified pilot, you skip the 3-week GCAA certification queue that stops most competitors. You are the product. The only question is which job type pays best, starts fastest, and builds toward something recurring.

That answer is solar inspection — anchored by the UAE's Clean Energy Strategy 2050, which mandates 100% clean energy by 2050. Every MW of solar installed creates a permanent, quarterly inspection requirement. The client base is institutional. The contracts are recurring. The sales cycle is weeks, not months.

Suggested Equity / Revenue Split (to discuss)
A common structure for this type of partnership: 60/40 or 50/50 revenue split — pilot takes the majority as the certified, insured operator providing the core deliverable. Business development partner receives equity stake or % for bringing the contracts, managing compliance, and building the client base. Exact split should reflect who carries the hardware cost and insurance risk. Recommend formalizing via a simple UAE partnership agreement or through the trade license structure from day one.
